Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

The short version

Jarvis runs on your own computer. The people who make Jarvis have no server that receives your data, and we never see, collect, sell or share it.

What stays on your computer

  • Your settings, your Groq key, what Jarvis remembers about you, your conversation history and any face you enrol are stored in the Jarvis folder on your computer (%APPDATA%\Jarvis on Windows, ~/Library/Application Support/Jarvis on Mac).
  • Your Google sign-in token, if you connect Google, is stored in the same folder. Uninstalling Jarvis does not delete this folder; delete it to remove everything.

What leaves your computer, and where it goes

  • Groq (groq.com) receives what you say to Jarvis, so it can turn your voice into text and write the answer. When you ask about your calendar or your email, the relevant events or messages are included in that request so Groq can answer. If you ask Jarvis to look at your screen, the picture of the window you choose is sent to Groq too. This happens with your own Groq key, under Groq's privacy policy.
  • Google: only if you connect your Google account. Jarvis then reads your Google Calendar and Gmail directly from Google.
  • Other services answer specific questions: weather, news, web search, maps, live cameras (YouTube) and updates (GitHub). They receive the search or place you asked about, not your personal data.

Google user data

If you connect your Google account, Jarvis asks for read-only access to your Google Calendar (calendar.readonly) and Gmail (gmail.readonly). Jarvis uses this access only to answer your own questions about your events and email, when you ask them. It does not send, change or delete email or events, and it does not save your email or events. Its own spoken answers are kept in the conversation history on your computer, so an answer that mentioned an email or event stays there until you clear it ("forget everything").

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Jarvis does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, does not use it to train AI models, and is not transferred to anyone except as needed to answer your request (the Groq request described above).

You can disconnect at any time: say "disconnect my Google account", or remove Jarvis at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Jarvis is not meant for children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue.
