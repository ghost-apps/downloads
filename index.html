Jarvis

A voice assistant for Windows and Mac. Say "Hey Jarvis" and ask: the time, the weather, the news, live city cameras, a 3D globe, your calendar and your email.

Download Jarvis

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, or a Mac (macOS 12 or newer).
  • A free Groq key. Jarvis shows you how to get one the first time it opens.
  • A microphone. A camera is optional (hand gestures and recognising you).

Google Calendar and Gmail

If you choose to connect your Google account, Jarvis can read your calendar and your email so it can answer questions like "what's on today?" or "any email from my bank?". It only reads; it never sends, changes or deletes anything. See the privacy policy for exactly what happens with that data.
